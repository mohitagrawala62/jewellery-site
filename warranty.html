Warranty & Authenticity

OUR GUARANTEE OF EXCELLENCE

Craftsmanship Guarantee

Every piece of ZOGGER jewelry is handcrafted with meticulous attention to detail. We provide a **6-month limited warranty** covering any manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship from the date of purchase.

WARRANTY VOID NOTICE: Any warranty claim is subject to the verification of our original **Security Seal**. If the seal was reported broken upon delivery or tampered with by an unauthorized third-party jeweler, the warranty will be considered null and void.

What is Covered?

  • Structural defects in the metal setting.
  • Issues with clasps, hinges, or earring backings.
  • Stone loosening due to setting errors (does not include stone loss).

What is Not Covered?

Our warranty does not cover issues arising from normal wear and tear, including:

  • Scratches, dents, or tarnishing due to chemical exposure (perfumes, lotions, etc.).
  • Lost or stolen jewelry.
  • Damage caused by accidental drops or improper storage.
  • Intentional alteration or repairs done outside of ZOGGER studios.
