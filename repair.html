Preserving Brilliance

HOW TO CHERISH YOUR ZOGGER PIECES

Avoid Chemicals

Apply perfumes, lotions, and hairsprays before putting on your jewelry. Chemicals can dull the shine of stones and metal.

Gentle Cleaning

Use a soft, lint-free cloth to wipe your jewelry after each wear. Avoid using harsh brushes or ultrasonic cleaners at home.

Proper Storage

Store your pieces in the original ZOGGER luxury box to avoid scratches. Keep them away from moisture and direct sunlight.
