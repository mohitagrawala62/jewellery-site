Shipping & Delivery

HANDLING YOUR TREASURES WITH CARE

Domestic Delivery

We offer complimentary insured shipping across India. Standard delivery typically takes **5-7 business days** for ready-to-ship pieces. Custom Bespoke or Bridal orders may take **5-10 business days** for crafting and delivery.

Secure Packaging

Every ZOGGER piece is housed in our signature tamper-proof luxury box and secured with a unique **security seal**. As per our policy, please inspect the seal upon arrival and do not accept the package if it appears tampered with.

Order Tracking

Once your jewelry is dispatched, you will receive a tracking ID via SMS and Email. You can also contact our Concierge directly on WhatsApp to get a real-time status update of your shipment.

Insurance

Your jewelry is fully insured by ZOGGER until it reaches your doorstep. We take 100% responsibility for the safety of your purchase during transit to ensure a worry-free experience.
